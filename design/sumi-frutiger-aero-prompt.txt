Use case: ui-mockup.
Create a high-fidelity portrait UI concept image for "Sumi", a native iOS manga reading app. The user specifically wants FRUTIGER AERO glassmorphism: refreshing aquatic cyan, sky blue, fresh grass green, luminous white, glossy transparent glass, clean water, airy sunlight. NO terracotta, orange or brown accents.
Show a single large straight-on iPhone library interface filling the portrait composition, minimal bezel, no perspective, no surrounding presentation text. A beautifully usable complete screen.
Visual direction: optimistic Frutiger Aero, translucent watery glass with rounded glossy beveled edges, subtle reflections, soft specular highlights, background visibly refracted and blurred through panels. A luminous sky-blue and turquoise backdrop with soft clouds, hints of lush green landscape, and gentle water caustics. Sophisticated and calm enough for reading, with clear dark blue text on light frosted surfaces. Nostalgic mid-2000s aquatic freshness combined with refined current iOS proportions. No neon cyberpunk, no dark charcoal aesthetic.
Layout: top small "Sumi" wordmark, round glass import plus button, large elegant "Library" heading. Frosted search capsule "Search your collection". One large beautiful featured manga artwork card showing an original anime traveler by a verdant lake under bright blue skies. Overlay a translucent bottom glass panel with small "CONTINUE READING", title "Blue Horizon", "Chapter 12", thin blue reading progress bar, and a glossy round blue play/reading button. Below, glass capsule segmented filter with "All", "Reading", "Unread", active state in fresh translucent blue. Section heading "On your shelf" and a row of three distinct beautiful original manga covers with compact titles. At bottom, floating pill-shaped glass tab bar with five crisp refined icons; Library selected in blue, other icons for discover, updates, downloads, settings. Respect safe areas, precise spacing, legible typography and accessible contrast.
Materials must look like actual clear/frosted aquatic glass with light transmission, subtle edge refraction and real depth, not opaque white rectangles. Distinct layered translucent floating controls over colorful scenery, restrained green accents. Beautiful polished interface design, not a wireframe. No watermarks or extraneous captions.
